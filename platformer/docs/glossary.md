### Glossary

This document contains terms and definitions that will be helpful in understanding 


* blit - fast image copying. It takes a bunch of pixels from one place (like an image or sprite) and pastes them onto another (like the screen or another image). "Blit" comes from BitBLT, which supposedly stands for Bit Boundary bLock Transfer. The acronym is... questionable. The "I" in blit doesn't mean anything - it just appeared in sometime in the 70's / 80's in as people started pronouncing "BLT". For more info see: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bit_blit and https://jargondb.org/glossary/blt
